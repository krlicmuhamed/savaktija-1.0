# Privacy

Savaktija does not collect, store, or transmit personal information or browsing
activity.

The extension runs on vaktija.ba pages to identify prayer-time cards and, only
after the user starts the switcharoo, temporarily animates and reorders those
cards in the page. Prayer labels and times remain part of the page and are not
sent anywhere. The extension does not use analytics, advertising, remote code,
or third-party services.

The extension requests no browser permissions.
